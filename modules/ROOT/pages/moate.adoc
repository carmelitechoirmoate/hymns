= Carmelite Church, Moate
:!page-pagination:

The choir sings at the Church of the Immaculate Conception, served by the small community of Carmelite friars at the *Carmelite Priory, Moate, Co. Westmeath*.
Full details are on the link:https://carmelites.ie/moate/[Carmelites in Moate website^].

== Confession, adoration and feasts

* *Confession:* Saturdays 3.00pm to 3.30pm. On any other day, ask at the shop.
* *Penitential services* in the week before Easter, before All Souls' Day and before Christmas. Confessions all day on Holy Saturday and Christmas Eve.
* *Eucharistic adoration:* Wednesdays 2.00pm to 7.00pm.
* *Our Lady of Mount Carmel Triduum:* 14, 15 and 16 July.
* *St Thérèse of Lisieux Triduum:* 29 September to 1 October. See what the choir sang at the xref:sundays/2025-09-29-therese-triduum.adoc[2025 Triduum].
* *Blessing of animals* on the feast of St Antony (17 January) and *blessing of throats* on St Blaise's day (3 February).

== A short history

* Carmelites first came to this area in the 14th century, at Ardnacranny, until the friary there was suppressed in 1540.
* Around 1770 land in the Newtown area of Moate was given to the Order, and a small friary and chapel were built. The present Carmelite presence dates from then.
* The friars ran schools in the town from the 1820s, and opened the Carmelite College in 1949. It became part of Moate Community School in 1996.
* The present church was completed in 1868 and dedicated to the Immaculate Conception of the Blessed Virgin Mary.
* The Pastoral Centre was built in 2004. The Repository sells religious goods, and Mass cards are available at the Friary.
