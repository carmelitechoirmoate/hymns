= Flos Carmeli
:composer: Attributed to St Simon Stock (13th century)
:hymnal-page: 
:key: 
:season: Marian, Carmelite feasts
:mass-part: Marian procession, Recessional

Flos Carmeli,
vitis florigera,
splendor coeli,
Virgo puerpera
singularis.

Mater mitis
sed viri nescia,
Carmelitis
esto propitia,
Stella maris.
