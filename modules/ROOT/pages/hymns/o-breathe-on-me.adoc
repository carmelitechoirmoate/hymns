= Breathe on Me, Breath of God
:composer: Words: Edwin Hatch
:hymnal-page: 
:key: 
:season: Pentecost, Any time
:mass-part: Anointing of the sick, Reflection
:sheet-music: o-breathe-on-me.jpg
:youtube: 
:audio: 
:hardbreaks-option:

*1.* Breathe on me, Breath of God,  fill me with life anew,  that I may love the
way you love,  and do what you would do.

*2.* Breathe on me, Breath of God,  until my heart is pure,  until my will is one
with yours,  to do and to endure.

*3.* Breathe on me, Breath of God,  so shall I never die,  but live with you the
perfect life  for all eternity
