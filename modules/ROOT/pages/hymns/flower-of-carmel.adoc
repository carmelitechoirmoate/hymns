= Flower of Carmel
:composer: English version of the Flos Carmeli, attributed to St Simon Stock
:hymnal-page: 
:key: 
:season: Marian, Carmelite feasts
:mass-part: Recessional, Marian procession
:sheet-music: flower-of-carmel.jpg

:audio: 
:hardbreaks-option:

Flower of Carmel,
Tall vine blossom laden;
Splendour of heaven,
Child-bearing yet maiden.
None equals thee. _(twice)_

Mother so tender,
whom no man didst know,
On Carmel's children
thy favours bestow;
Star of the Sea! _(twice)_

O gentle Mother
who in Carmel reigns,
Share with your servants
that gladness you gained
and now enjoy. _(twice)_

Hail, Gate of Heaven,
with glory now crowned,
Bring us to safety
where thy Son is found,
true joy to see. _(twice)_
