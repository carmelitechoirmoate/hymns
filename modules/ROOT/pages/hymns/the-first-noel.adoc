All The First Noel, the Angels did say
as to certain poor shepherds in fields as they lay
Men In fields where they lay keeping their sheep
On a cold winter's night that was so deep.
All Noel, Noel, Noel, Noel
Born is the King of Israel!

All They looked up and saw a star
Shining in the East beyond them far
And to the earth it gave great light
And so it continued both day and night.
Ladies Noel, Noel, Noel, Noel
All Born is the King of Israel!
