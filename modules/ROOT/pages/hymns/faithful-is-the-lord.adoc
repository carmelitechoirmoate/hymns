= Faithful Is the Lord

_Refrain_
Faithful is the Lord our King,
let us all in glory sing,
ever sing with sweet accord:
Holy, holy, holy Lord.

God of life and God of seed,
God of nations, lands and creeds,
God of heaven, God of earth,
God made man in lowly birth.

_Refrain_

God of stars and God of seas,
God of harvest, God of wheat,
God of hillside, God of field,
God of love in Christ revealed.

_Refrain_

God the Father, God the Son,
God the Spirit, Three in One,
God who dwells in every heart,
God ever faithful, be not far.

_Refrain_
