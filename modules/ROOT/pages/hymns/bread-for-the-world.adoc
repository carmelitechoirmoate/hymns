Bread for the world
A world of hunger
Wine for all peoples
People who thirst
May we who eat
Be bread for others
May we who drink
Pour out our love
Lord Jesus Christ
You are the bread of life
Broken to reach and heal the wounds
Of human pain
Where we divide Your people
You are waiting there
On bended knee
To wash our feet with endless care
Bread for the world
A world of hunger
Wine for all peoples
People who thirst
May we who eat
Be bread for others
May we who drink
Pour out our love
Lord Jesus Christ
You are the wine of peace (ooh)
Poured into hearts once broken
And where dryness sleeps
Where we are tired and weary (ooh)
You are waiting there
To be the way which beckons us
Beyond despair
